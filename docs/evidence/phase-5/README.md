## Acceptance criteria 2, 3, 5 on testnet: what was and was not achieved

The 2 hour live run was **not completed**: the testnet MON ran out (about 0.07 MON per executed
order, 0.1 MON per round of maintenance; deployer and keeper wallets ended at 0.28 and 0.04 MON).
The numbers below are what the shorter runs measured; none of them is a substitute for the 2 hour
run, and the criteria stay open in the report.

Injected shock (final code, 2026-10-06 15:49:38.837 UTC, +2 % on both sources for 20 s):

| Step | Time (UTC) | Block |
|---|---|---|
| shock injected at the relay | 15:49:38.837 | |
| keeper detects (`SOURCE_DIVERGENCE`, the two sources do not jump in the same instant; then `PRICE_SHOCK`) | 15:49:38.948 (+0.11 s) | 68724938 (its head at that moment) |
| `haltQuoting` mined | 15:49:39.825 (+0.88 s after detection) | 68724942 |
| quotes back (`unhaltQuoting`, after the 20 s shock and the 15 s hysteresis) | | 68725105 |

By head numbering that is **4 blocks**, in wall-clock about **1.0 s from the shock to the halt being
mined**: 0.11 s relay and detection, about 0.38 s in the keeper before the broadcast (fees, nonce and
signing round trips; pre-warming was added after this run), and 0.50 s from broadcast to inclusion (the ledger's
`latencyMs` for that halt is 496). The 2-block target is met on anvil (chaos tests, also with 150 ms
added to every RPC call) and **not met on Monad testnet**: a transaction takes 0.5 to 1.5 s to be
included there (the ledger's inclusion p50 is 1.0 to 1.5 s across kinds), i.e. 1 to 4 blocks.

Quote age (blocks between the first block at or after an order's second and the block that
executed it): 9 and 10 blocks in run A, 6 in run B (n = 3). The breakdown of the 6 in run B,
milliseconds after the pricing block was seen: order known -1232, execution started +101, simulated
+441, broadcast +749 (308 ms of that for fees, nonce and signing; pre-warming and a parallel
simulation were added after this run), mined +1851. So 0.75 s of the 1.85 s is the keeper and 1.1 s
is the chain's inclusion: even with the keeper at zero, about 3 blocks is the floor. The venue's own
limit is `maxLateness` = 4 s (10 blocks): run A's 9 and 10 blocks were one block from an expired
order, which is why the fast path was reordered and the simulation parallelised (see the report).
