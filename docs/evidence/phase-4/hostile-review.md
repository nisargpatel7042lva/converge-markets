# Phase 4 hostile review log

Reviewer: an independent subagent in a senior smart-contract auditor persona (did not write the code; read-only on the repo, PoCs and about 45 mutants in a scratch copy, no secrets read, nothing broadcast). One pass on the post-redesign code (settlement bound to the epoch end). Its PoC tests lived in its scratch copy; the tests they motivated are in `contracts/test/unit/VaultAudit.t.sol` and `ForwardVenue.t.sol` (`test_audit_*`).

**Verdict: 0 CRITICAL, 2 HIGH, 6 MEDIUM, the rest LOW/INFO.** No path was found where LP funds are stolen or permanently locked without a privileged role.

| ID | Sev | Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-01 | HIGH | Marks pinned to the epoch end but the inventory read at settlement time: a fill after the end (priced later) was valued at the earlier price. PoC: a settler gained 11.7 USDC (1.3%) on a 900-share redemption | **Fixed.** `venueFill` and `venueView` refuse trading while the epoch that just ended has requests and can still be settled (`_settlementPending`). Tests: `test_audit_F01_fillsAreFrozenWhileAnEndedEpochAwaitsSettlement`, `test_audit_F01_noFreezeWithoutRequests_orAfterTheWindow`; mutants (freeze removed in either place) killed |
| F-02 | HIGH | The "loss capped at 1% per market, 8% total" claim is false in practice: (a) a colluding keeper (sigma) and taker take about 1% per market per round, up to 8% in a burst, (b) flatten-and-resplit forgot realised loss (3 cycles realised 2.1% against a 1% ceiling), (c) the breaker is the only stop and `resumeQuoting` reset its baseline | **(b) fixed**: a market's basis and cash survive pruning, so realised loss stays on record (`test_audit_F02_realisedLossSurvivesFlatteningAndResplit`, mutant killed). **(c) fixed**: resume keeps the day's baseline (`test_breaker_tripsOnDrawdownAndNeverBlocksExits`). **(a) not removable on chain**: it is the keeper's one lever. Claim corrected in ADR-005 and the threat model (the ceilings bound OPEN worst-case loss; realised loss is cumulative per market; extraction by a colluding keeper is about 1% per market per round, up to the 8% cap in a burst before the first auto-checkpoint, then stopped by the breaker). The testnet deploy uses a tight sigma band 0.4 to 1.2. **Residual risk R3, needs Nisarg: key hygiene for the keeper and the band per asset** |
| F-03 | MED | An executor could wait up to `maxLateness` (30 s) and then decide whether to execute: an option worth the price drift | **Fixed.** `MAX_LATENESS = 10` (constructor rejects more), deployed with 4 s; `test_audit_F03_executionAfterTheLatenessWindowReverts`, `test_audit_spreadFloorAndLatenessCap`; mutant killed. The 4 s option is a small residual (state it, not zero) |
| F-04 | MED | `mergeInventory` keeper-only: a dead keeper locks up to 50% of NAV in pairs and throttles exits | **Fixed.** Keeper, owner and guardian can always merge, anyone while quoting is paused (`test_audit_F04_mergeCanNeverBeKeptHostage`, mutant killed) |
| F-05 | MED | Owner setters (keeper, sigma band, params, risk) have no timelock | **Partly fixed, rest accepted.** Spread floor 0.02 added. Owner must be a TimelockController behind the Safe on mainnet (Phase 9): the contract keeps instant tightening and pause. Not fixed in code |
| F-06 | MED | A registered market with zero tokens could not be pruned: a taker could burn registry slots | **Fixed.** `redeemResolved` drops an empty resolved market instead of reverting; `pruneEmpty` is permissionless (`test_audit_F06_pruneEmptyFreesTheSlot`) |
| F-07 | MED | Mutation survivors (AE total-cap, TooLate, AX, AW, AS, BB, BC) | **Fixed.** Tests added (`test_audit_F07_*`, `test_rooms_freeBranchesAreAllowedAtZeroCeiling`, the F-02/F-03 tests); all ten mutants killed (`mutation-checks.md`). Not covered: `nonReentrant` removal on `venueFill` (the vault's callees are trusted) and the tau fallback in `_upBand` |
| F-08 | LOW | The post-trade loss check was a tautology | **Fixed.** The ceiling is struck on the pre-trade position, the exact loss after the trade must respect it |
| F-09 | LOW | The vault stores venue-supplied `refPrice` as the last mark | **Accepted, documented.** The venue is trusted at the level of its caps and replacement is timelocked; the value only feeds the breaker and can only lower the sizing NAV |
| F-10 | LOW | Omitted-report marks had no age bound; `checkpoint` can raise the sizing NAV | **Partly fixed.** A last mark older than 1 hour is no information (1/2 plus or minus the band). `checkpoint` raising the sizing NAV is accepted (a caller can only choose which assets to omit, and every value is a verified mark) |
| F-11 | LOW | The sigma step limit is bypassable by letting sigma go stale | **Documented.** The band, not the step, is the control |
| F-12 | LOW | `renounceOwnership` one-step | **Fixed** (`test_audit_F12_ownershipCanNotBeRenounced`) |
| F-13 | LOW | Fork test used a selector-less `expectRevert`; fee-payload dependency | **Fixed**: it now expects the real proxy's `VerifierNotFound(bytes32(0))`. The fee-mode dependency is documented (ADR-005 consequences) |
| F-14 | INFO | Gas claim understated | **Measured.** 16 registered markets all holding excess: `settleEpoch` 725,675 gas, `executeOrder` including the auto re-valuation 1,138,252 (forge); 590,921 on Monad testnet with one market |
| F-15 | INFO | Hygiene: `cancelVenue` event, threat-model cross-references, plan text | **Fixed** |

Claims in the first ADR/threat-model draft that the review showed unsupported are corrected in the documents (settlement independence, the post-trade check, the cap wording, "owner cannot move funds", merge in P4, gas).

**Exit:** no open CRITICAL; the two HIGH are fixed (F-01) or fixed-with-claim-corrected-and-residual-stated (F-02a). One MEDIUM (F-05) is open by decision (operational mitigation). No second review pass was run on the fixes; the mutation checks and the new tests are the evidence.
